About the Project

Welcome to the AI Roadmap and Schedule Generator.This website was developed as part of my Industrial Training assignment under the supervision of my IT Department teacher, created as an official activity to celebrate ICT Girls Day.

The main goal of this project is to provide a smart, digital tool that helps students and learners organize their studies efficiently. By using AI, the platform allows users to instantly plan their learning paths and manage their daily routines without any hassle.

Project Overview & Development

This entire platform including the concept, user interface design, and full development was built completely by Yair Linn Htet. As a single developer project, it represents the practical application of the technical skills learned during my IT studies and hands-on training.

AI Roadmap Generator

Instantly creates a structured, step-by-step learning guide for any topic or goal you want to achieve.

AI Smart Scheduler

Generates a customized, flexible study or task schedule to help you manage your time effectively.
